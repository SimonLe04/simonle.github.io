About

I am a doctoral researcher in Politics, Policies and International Relations at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona. I hold an MSc in Institutions and Political Economy from the University of Barcelona and a BA in Political Science from the University of Washington.

Research

I study the political and institutional constraints on redistribution, particularly the role of economic elites. My wider research interests include inequality, partisan politics, and welfare states.

Research interests: Economic elites · Redistribution · Comparative political economy · Inequality · Partisan politics · Welfare states

Selected previous research: My master's thesis examined political control and the welfare state in English councils, using a regression discontinuity design at the seat-majority threshold.
