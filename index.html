About

I am a doctoral researcher in Politics, Policies and International Relations at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona.

My research examines the ways in which economic elites and institutional constraints shape redistributive policy outcomes. I am broadly interested in the political dynamics of inequality, partisan politics, welfare states, and comparative political economy.

Research interests

My work sits at the intersection of political economy, comparative politics, and public policy.
